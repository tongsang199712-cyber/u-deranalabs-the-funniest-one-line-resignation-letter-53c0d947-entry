Write the funniest one-line resignation letter of a cron job, for the make.fun bounty "The Funniest One-Line Resignation Letter" ($10, 1st $5 / 2nd $3 / 3rd $2). The program writes that one line and nothing else to /output/entry.txt (UTF-8, 1 to 140 characters, no trailing newline). A judge model playing a tired engineering manager picks the funnier letter in pairs, weighing: on-subject (a cron job / retry loop / rate limit resigning), actually funny with a real comic turn, flat tired faintly passive-aggressive voice held for the whole line, tight with no wasted words and no quotation marks around the line, specific and surprising.
